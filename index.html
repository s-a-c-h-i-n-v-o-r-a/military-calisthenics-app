Military Calisthenics Daily

52 · 5'5.5" · 149 → <145 lbs · Muscle retention + fitness

Daily Structure (30 min)

5–7 min Warm-up / mobility
15 min Calisthenics circuit (2 rounds)
8–10 min Yoga / cool-down

6 days/week. One lighter day if needed. Progress by adding time, reps, or harder variations.

Nutrition note

Training supports muscle. Weight drop to <145 needs a mild calorie deficit + ~110–120 g protein/day. Track weight consistently.

1. Warm-up (5–7 min)

Cat-Cow

On hands and knees. Arch back and lift head, then round spine and tuck chin. Slow.

Plus: arm circles, leg swings, 10 easy squats, world's greatest stretch.

2. Circuit (15 min · 2 rounds · 40s work / 20s rest)

Ready

3. Yoga / Mobility (8–10 min)

Downward Dog

Hips high, inverted V. Press hands, soft knees ok.

Warrior II

Wide stance, front knee bent, arms out, gaze over front hand.

Also: pigeon or figure-4 hip opener, child’s pose, breathing.
